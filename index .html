Why ScriptIQ Exists

This is not technology
built from theory

Elizabeth Olalere spent 15+ years working across Nollywood productions — managing schedules, coordinating departments, breaking down scripts by hand, line by line, scene by scene.

She worked on Netflix productions, cinema releases, and independent films. She experienced firsthand the hours wasted on manual pre-production workflows that should take minutes.

Missolowo ScriptIQ was created to help filmmakers save time, reduce production stress, and automate pre-production workflows using AI.

Elizabeth Olalere
Film Producer · Production Manager · FilmTech Founder
Elizabeth Olalere is a Film Producer, Production Manager, and FilmTech Founder with over 15 years of experience in the film industry. Having worked across multiple production departments including Producing, Production Management, Production Coordination, Assistant Directing, Welfare Coordination, Craft Coordination, and Stage Production, she experienced firsthand the challenges of manual pre-production workflows. After working on Netflix productions, cinema releases, YouTube productions, and independent films, she recognised the need for a simpler, smarter way to plan productions. Missolowo ScriptIQ was created to help filmmakers save time, reduce production stress, and automate pre-production workflows using AI.
