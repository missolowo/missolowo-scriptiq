Five AI-powered tools that replace hours of manual prep work — built specifically for how African productions actually operate.

01
📋
AI Script Breakdown
Paste any screenplay. Instantly extract cast, props, locations, wardrobe, SFX, vehicles and extras — scene by scene. Export to CSV or JSON for your team.
Live Now

📄
AI Call Sheet Generator
Auto-generate professional call sheets from your script breakdown. Cast, crew, locations and timing — all formatted and ready to share with your team.
Coming Soon

Coming Soon
