AI Script Breakdown
Upload any screenplay and instantly generate cast breakdown, props breakdown, costume breakdown, location breakdown, equipment requirements and production notes — scene by scene. Includes scene number, INT/EXT and DAY/NIGHT for every scene. Unlimited scenes. Export to PDF, CSV or JSON.

Why ScriptIQ Exists

This is not technology
built from theory

Elizabeth Olalere has spent 15+ years working across film and media productions — and 6 years as a Production Manager — managing schedules, coordinating departments, breaking down scripts by hand, line by line, scene by scene.

She worked on Netflix productions, cinema releases, and independent films. She experienced firsthand the hours wasted on manual pre-production workflows that should take minutes.

Missolowo ScriptIQ was created to help filmmakers save time, reduce production stress, and automate pre-production workflows using AI.
