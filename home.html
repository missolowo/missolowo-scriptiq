Why Missolowo Slate exists

Elizabeth Olalere

Selected credits — Aníkúlápó · Jagun Jagun 1&2 · Lisabi 1&2 · Ijogbon · 7 Doors

A producer who codes. A builder who tells stories.

For years, I watched talented filmmakers spend more time managing paperwork than creating films. Script breakdowns took days. Shooting schedules were built manually. Call sheets were created under pressure, often late at night. Every production started with unnecessary chaos.

After experiencing these challenges on real film sets, I realised the industry didn't need another generic productivity tool. It needed software that truly understood how filmmakers work.

Every feature inside Missolowo Slate is inspired by real production experience, real challenges, and real conversations with filmmakers. It's my contribution to the future of filmmaking.
